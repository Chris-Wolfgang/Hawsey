MAUI: a compact layout for phones (#952). Where the table would scale below three-quarters of its size, as on a phone in landscape, the page shows the game at full size instead: a top bar with the buttons, tricks, score and trump; the trick in the middle with a chip for each seat; and the hand with 48 units of each card showing to tap. Tablets keep the table, and the desktop keeps it at any window size (it scales to fit). A Layout button switches between the two on any device. The bid, trump, Hawsey-exchange, round-summary and log panels are no longer scaled with the table, and their buttons are 48 high on phones and tablets. The bid panel is centred, clear of the hand.
