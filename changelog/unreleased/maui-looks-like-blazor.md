type: feature

The MAUI app looks and plays like the Blazor app. It has the felt table with trump watermarks, the tricks and score panels, the partner and both opponents with their face-down cards, dealer puck and bid badges, and the trick laid out by seat with who led and the winning card. Your hand is sorted by suit and can be rearranged by dragging. A round ends in a summary with Next round (Play again at game over), and a game log is one click away.
