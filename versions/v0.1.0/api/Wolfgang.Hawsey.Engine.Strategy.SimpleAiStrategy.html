Class SimpleAiStrategy

Namespace

Assembly

The AI player every Hawsey UI uses for its computer seats (through GameSession). It bids on hand strength (trump length, jacks in trump and aces outside it), names the suit that scores best, leads a card nothing left can beat when it has one, lets its partner's winning card stand, and otherwise wins as cheaply as it can or throws its lowest card.

Remarks

Passes when the hand is worth less than the minimum bid, or when its bid (at most 11) wouldn't beat HighBid. As the dealer with every other player passed, it bids just the minimum: there is nothing to outbid.
